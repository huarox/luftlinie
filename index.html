You know how far. Not where.

Someone hides a spot on the map. Everyone else sees only the straight-line distance, updating as they move. First one there wins.

No account. Works on foot or from the passenger seat.

How a round goes

  1. Hide the target. The host taps a public spot on the map, or lets Luftlinie pick a road or path at the right distance.
  2. Share the code. Friends open the link or type six characters. No sign-up.
  3. Follow the number. Walk or ride. The distance shrinks or grows. Read it, turn, try again.
